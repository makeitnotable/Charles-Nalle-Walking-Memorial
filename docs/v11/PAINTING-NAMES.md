Source: Wil's PDF of <https://markapriest.org/charles-nalle-series/>, 22 Aug 2026.
(The site itself is blocked by this environment's egress proxy; the PDF's text
layer and its six rendered pages are the source.)

plate beside the file on disk and comparing the picture.

**The two gaps.** Neither of these appears anywhere in the six pages:

- `mansion/horizontal` — a dark interior, two men bent over papers.
- `barbershop/horizontal` — a white horse rearing, a US flag, storefront signage
  reading "CLOTH[ES] FOR S…" and "WEST TROY". The page carries an *Escape to
  West Troy* **pen** drawing (a rowboat on water) but no acrylic of that name,
  and the scenes do not match.

Titles are not applied to any of the ten until those two are known — naming
eight works correctly and leaving two under location names would read as an
error on the same page.

## 2 · A caution about the source

The artist's page has at least one caption offset by one. On page 4 the drawing
whose **burned-in** label reads "Rushing the Room, Pen on paper 8½ × 7⅛, 2008"
is captioned "Martin Struck by Deputy Sheriff Morrison" underneath. Where a
plate carries its own burned-in label, that label was taken as the truth.

## 3 · The studies — item 8's answer

The "sketches" are the series' pen-on-paper works, each a titled piece in its
own right. Verified against the same source:

| site key | what it actually is | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `bakery/sketch` | **Captured at Holeur's Fashionable Bakery** — pen, 8.5″ × 7.0625″, 2008 | ✅ correct — pairs with *Bakery Abduction* |
| `commissioners-office/sketch` | **Don't Let Them Have Him!** — pen, 8″ × 7⅛″, 2008 | ❌ **wrong** — this is the study for the painting the **ferry** chapter shows |
| `commissioners-office/sketch-pt2` | **The Altruist** — pen on paper | ✅ correct — pairs with *The Altruist* |
| `mansion/sketch` | a genteel street: a mansion, period dress, a man with a horse | not on the series page |
| `ferry/sketch` | a dense brawl on cobbles among rowhouses | not on the series page |
| `barbershop/sketch` | figures dragging a man past a cart | not on the series page |

So Wil is right, and it is more specific than "some are wrong": **Part 2's study
is correct and Part 1's is not.** It is not a pt1/pt2 swap either — pt2 is
already right. The drawing sitting beside *1st & State Street* is a real Nalle
drawing, just of a different moment, and the painting it belongs to is the one
the ferry chapter hangs.

**Not changed, deliberately.** Fixing it means deciding what should hang beside
*1st & State Street* — show nothing, move the drawing to the ferry chapter and
displace what is there now, or supply the study for *1st & State Street*, which
is not in `masters/` (no studies were delivered) and not on the series page.
Removing a study is as much a decision as replacing one.
