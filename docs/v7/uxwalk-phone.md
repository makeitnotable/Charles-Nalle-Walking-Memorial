## What already feels excellent
- The chapter typographic system: numbered "(01) LISTEN / (02) HISTORY / (03) THE MORAL" sections, Caslon display against the serif body, hanging pull-quotes with attribution — calm and legible at every phone width, zero horizontal overflow at 320.
- The audio player and its mini-player: big 56px play, clear duration, latches to a bottom-left 44px pill when you scroll away — exactly the right restraint.
- The museum approach on a phone: the painting stays fully visible above the plaque, the "Back returns to the hall" hint respects the platform, and the reduced-motion fallback degrades to a clean grid.
- QR arrival on a chapter reads instantly ("SPOT 01 OF 5 · TROY, NY · APRIL 27, 1860"), 48px CTAs everywhere, the 404 page copy, and the map lens caption/credit are all considered.
