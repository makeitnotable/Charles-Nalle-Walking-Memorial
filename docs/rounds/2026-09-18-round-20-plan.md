## The three asks

1. **The Take the Walk offset he chose on the device** — the `?cta=-4` link —
   becomes the default.
2. **The chapter cards inherit the same vertical spacing** in the view that
   shows them after Take the Walk (or a pin tap).
3. **No scrolling down into the next section** once the walk has been taken:
   "disable scroll in this spot".
