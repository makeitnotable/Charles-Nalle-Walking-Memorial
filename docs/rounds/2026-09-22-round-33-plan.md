## 1 · What the shots measure

The screenshots are the Pixel's own pixels — 1080×2400 at a device pixel
ratio of 2.625, so 1 CSS px = 2.625 device px — and were read row by row
(`scratch: measure.py`, a stdlib PNG reader; luminance runs per column band).
Everything below is from the screen's bottom edge, in CSS px.

| Shot | What | Bottom edge at | Rule that put it there |
|---|---|---|---|
| all four | the gesture pill (white, 10 device px tall) | top 13.7, bottom 9.9 | Android |
| splash | the frame's rounded border | 10.7 | `p-2.5` on a `100dvh` box |
| splash | the WALK THE STORY button | 38.1 | 10 + `max(28px, env(safe-area-inset-bottom))` = 10 + 28 |
| map, lens open | the BACK TO TODAY door's box | **24.0** | `--ui-inset` = `max(20px, env(safe-area-inset-bottom))` |
| Bakery hero | the title's ink (BAKERY) | 23.2 | `--gutter` (20) + the line box's slack |
| museum rail | the 01 / 10 counter's ink | 28.6 | `--ui-inset` (24) + the line box's slack |

Three facts follow, none of them inferred:

1. **Chrome draws the page to the screen's bottom edge.** The `100dvh` frame's
   border sits 10.7px from the edge, under the pill; the hero (`100svh`) ends
   exactly at the edge. This is Chrome for Android 135+ on Android 15+ with
   gesture navigation, edge to edge, which the site opts into with
   `viewport-fit=cover` (needed on iOS since v9 for the same reason).
2. **Chrome reports the gesture bar as `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, and it is
   24px on his phone.** The door's box lands at 24.0 and the counter's ink at
   28.6 (= 24 + the 12px meta line's 4.6px of half-leading and descent) — both
   consumers of `--ui-inset`, whose `max(20, 24)` is 24. The bar is reported the
   whole time the address bar is at the top, so nothing moves on scroll here.
3. **The pill is the floor the eye reads, and the lane stands ON it.** Every
   bottom control's box sits at the bar's top edge, 10px above the pill; the
   hero title (on the raw gutter) and the splash frame (10px) sit inside the
   bar. On his iPhone the same code is right: at rest the toolbar is the floor,
   the layout viewport ends at its top edge and the lane sits 16–38px above it;
   collapsed, the inset is 34 and 34 is already 21px clear of the indicator
   (playbook §1). The `max()` was measured on iOS and is wrong only where a bar
   is drawn over the page with no toolbar between — Android's gesture bar.

## 2 · What ships

**One rule: where a browser draws a gesture bar over the page, the bottom lane
is the gutter ABOVE the bar. Everywhere else, nothing changes.**

- `global.css`: two registered tokens next to `--ui-inset`. `--gesture-bar`
  (0px) and `--ui-inset-b` (`var(--ui-inset)`) are set in `:root`; under
  `@supports not (-webkit-touch-callout: none)` — the inverse of the gate every
  iOS-only rule uses, so CSS decides, never the user agent — `--gesture-bar` is
  `env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px)` and `--ui-inset-b` is `max(var(--ui-inset),
  calc(var(--gutter) + var(--gesture-bar)))`. On iOS the block never matches
  and the two `:root` lines are the whole story (`--ui-inset-b` IS
  `--ui-inset`, 20 at rest, 34 collapsed — untouched). Wherever no bar is
  reported (every desktop, three-button Android, older Chrome, this
  container) `gutter + 0 ≤ --ui-inset` and the `max()` returns `--ui-inset`:
  byte for byte today's values. On his Pixel: `--ui-inset` 24 (unchanged),
  `--ui-inset-b` 44, `--gesture-bar` 24.
- Every bottom-anchored control takes the bottom lane. `--map-lane` (the walk
  door and the card strip; TroyMap's camera reads it), the Mapbox controls'
  bottom padding, the 1858 lens shell's, the map's scroll handle and hint
  chip, the desktop zoom notice; the museum's dot rail, the plaque drawer's
  peek header (`+1px`) and body (`+8px`), the card's and the menu panel's
  height caps; the narration mini-player; the unused bottom-right menu
  variant. The camera's label safe box and the short-viewport fit take the
  lane for their floor (`insetB`), so the pins clear the door where it rises.
- The two things not on the lane take the bar directly: the splash's outer box
  gets `padding-bottom: 10px (20 from 768) + --gesture-bar`, so the frame ends
  10px above the bar as it ends 10px above Safari's toolbar, and the CTA keeps
  its 28 inside the frame; the hero lockup's inset becomes `gutter +
  --gesture-bar` on phones (and `+ --gesture-bar` on the tablet and desktop
  tiers), so the title's ink sits 22–23px above the bar — round 23's rule
  ("the same as the gutter") with the bar as the floor.
- Nothing at the top or the sides moves. The corner menu is on `--menu-inset`
  (20 at every bar); the map's top doors, the Skip button, the drawer's side
  padding stay on `--ui-inset`, 24 on his Pixel exactly as before this round.
- `/glass-lab` prints `--ui-inset`, `--ui-inset-b` and `--gesture-bar` in its
  gates block, so the numbers can be read on any phone.

On his Pixel, from the screen's edge: the splash frame 34 and its CTA 62 (38
above the bar, as 38 above the toolbar on iOS); the walk door 40 and the cards
40 (16 above the bar, the lane he chose in round 20); the Mapbox logo and (i)
44; the lens's door 44; the hero title's ink ≈ 46–47; the dot rail 44 and the
counter's ink ≈ 49; the drawer's peek title ink 45; the mini-player 44. The
pill's top is 13.7, so the smallest gap from any control to the pill is now
26px (was 10), and to the bar's top edge 16–20px (was 0).

## 4 · What only the phones can show

- **The Pixel.** That Chrome reports 24 (`/glass-lab`, the gates block, prints
  it) and that the composition reads balanced to him: the frame, the door, the
  title and the rail a gutter above the bar. The bar's own paint over the page
  is Android's; nothing here changes it.
- **The iPhone.** That nothing moved. By construction nothing can — the
  `@supports not` block does not match on iOS, and every consumer reads a token
  that equals the old one there — but it is his phone that proves it, and the
  routes he signed off (rounds 21, 24, 28–29) are the ones to glance at.
- **A tablet with a gesture bar** (Pixel Tablet, Chrome): the same rule with
  the 40px gutter — 64 from the edge. Not measured on a device.
- **Chrome with its address bar at the bottom**: the page reaches the gesture
  bar only when that toolbar collapses, so Chrome's inset changes with it and
  the lane follows (20 → 44), the counterpart of the 14px shift iOS already
  has (playbook §10). Not measured on a device.
