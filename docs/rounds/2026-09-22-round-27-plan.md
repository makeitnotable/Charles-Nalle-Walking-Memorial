## Diagnosis

The lens shell was `absolute inset-0` on the UI layer — the box the reader
sees at rest. The canvas is bigger: T above it under the address bar, E below
it under the toolbar (the runways, rounds 9 and 17), and the bars are glass.
The wash never reached the runways, so the bars showed the undimmed map. The
same class of trap as round 19's framing: sized to the visible box when it
should be sized to the canvas.
