## 1 · Why it was late, and why it was abrupt

The X was mounted by the drawer's React state (`sheet === "full"`), and on
his phone that state is set by round 28's scroll path only once the scroll
settles — 160ms after the last scroll event, which on iOS is after the
flick's momentum has run out. The drawer itself was already up (its position
is applied per event), so the X arrived about a second later. And it arrived
with no transition: the header grew by the button's box in one frame.

## 2 · What ships

- **Mounted in both states, folded in peek.** The X exists whenever the
  drawer does. In peek it is folded away — no height, no bottom margin, no
  stroke, no opacity, scaled to 0.6 — so the peek header is exactly as tall
  as v14.2 left it ("in peek the title stands alone"). Focusable and exposed
  to assistive tech in the open state only.
- **It unfolds with the drawer.** `applySheet` marks the sheet `data-x` the
  moment the drawer's position passes 12% of its travel (cleared again under
  8%), whatever moves it — the finger, the wheel, or the page's scroll — and
  the fold is a CSS transition over the house 300ms at the house curve:
  height, margin, stroke, opacity and scale together, the title sliding down
  under it. A flick to full and the unfold finish together; a slow drag
  shows the X arriving as the drawer rises. Under reduced motion the
  site-wide clamp makes it a cut.
- **Travel measured without the X.** The drawer's mapping from position to
  pixels divides by its travel — the sheet less the header — and the header
  is now taller by the X's box while it is unfolded. Measured to the header
  without the X, the mapping never jumps as it unfolds under a moving
  finger; the two inline copies of that arithmetic (the stage swipe and the
  wheel) use the one `sheetTravel()` now.
- **The state follows at the ends at once.** On the scroll path the state
  snaps to full the moment the position reaches 1 (and to peek at 0), as it
  does on the wheel, so nothing that hangs on the state waits for the
  settle.
- **A latent trap, found by the instrument.** The sheet's resize observer
  (the dot rail's fallback height) re-applied the drawer's position from the
  state's end — 1 or 0 — on every resize. With the X unfolding inside the
  header during a drag, the sheet's box moved by a fraction and the observer
  threw the drawer from 0.41 back to 0 under a moving finger. It re-applies
  the live position now, and `approach` resets that position, so a remount
  still lands where the state says.

## 4 · Device pass — what to look at

Tap a painting. Swipe the drawer up slowly: the X should grow in as the
drawer rises, the title sliding down under it. Flick it: drawer and X should
arrive together. Swipe it down: the X folds as the drawer settles. FAIL: the
X still arriving late, or popping in; the drawer jumping as the X appears;
the title's position in peek changed.
