## 1 · What moved it

Skip is the hall's top-left control, positioned on `--ui-inset` — the lane
every corner control shares, a `max()` of the gutter and **all four**
safe-area insets. On his phone `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` is 0 while
Safari's bars are expanded and 34px once they collapse (the home indicator
comes out from under the toolbar; playbook §1), so `--ui-inset` reads 20px
at rest and 34px with the bars away, and Skip sat 14px further right and
14px further down every time the bars went, and came back every time they
returned.

A pan on the hall moves the bars. The canvas keeps `touch-action: pan-y` in
the hall (a vertical drag is the walk — a page scroll), so any pan with
vertical drift scrolls the page a little, Safari collapses its bars on the
scroll, and Skip jumps. Panning back expands them and Skip jumps back. That
is "moves its position when panning around". Nothing in rounds 24–31
touched Skip's markup; the shift is round 23's burger symptom ("position is
getting pushed") on the other top corner, which round 23 fixed for the menu
alone ("No other corner control changes") and the playbook has carried as an
open item since (§10: "Wil's call if it ever reads as jitter"). He has
called it.

Measured here (Chromium, the collapsed bars' safe area emulated through
CDP, the stage pinned at rail 0.4): Skip's box 20/20 → 34/34 with the
bottom inset at 34, while the top-right menu, on round 23's `--menu-inset`,
held. Nothing else a pan does moves it: a horizontal drag (the look, past
the "looked away" threshold that re-renders the chrome), a vertical drag
(the walk, page scrolled, rail moved), a diagonal drag, a scripted scroll,
the viewport at 753 (the bars minimized) — Skip identical through all of
them, before the fix as after.

## 2 · What ships

- **Skip reads its own inset.** `--skip-inset` on the stage: the gutter,
  the top inset and the left inset — `max(var(--gutter),
  env(safe-area-inset-top, 0px), env(safe-area-inset-left, 0px))`, round
  23's rule for the top-right menu mirrored for the top-left corner. A
  corner needs only its own two edges, and nothing at the bottom edge can
  move it. Skip's `top` and `left` use it in place of `--ui-inset`; the
  formula is otherwise unchanged (`top` still adds the top safe area, as
  it did).
- **Nothing else moves.** The chip row's band is measured from Skip's box
  each layout, so it holds with Skip. The bottom-centre column — Face
  forward and the dot rail — still rides the bottom lane (round 33's
  `--ui-inset-b`, which is `--ui-inset` on iOS), where the bottom inset is
  the one that matters. The menu keeps `--menu-inset`. The bottom-right
  menu variant, unused, keeps `--ui-inset`.
- **Identical wherever no safe area is set.** Desktop, tablet, Android, and
  the phone with the bars at rest: `max(gutter, 0, 0)` is the gutter, the
  same number `--ui-inset` gave — so the visual gate's shots cannot change,
  and the only pixel that changes anywhere is the one he reported.
- **A permanent instrument.** `npm run qa:skip` (`scripts/museum-skip.mjs`)
  drives the hall with CDP touch and asserts Skip's box identical through a
  look drag (Face forward appears, the page does not scroll), a walk drag
  (the page scrolls, the rail moves), a diagonal drag, a 120px scroll, the
  viewport at 753, and the bottom inset at 34 — with the bars stood in and
  without — while the dot rail moves with the bottom lane by design (14px
  on iOS; in Chromium the lane is the gutter above the bar, round 33) and
  the menu holds; and at 1440/768 that Skip sits on the gutter and equals
  `--ui-inset`.

Two rules in CSS, two tokens in one inline style, one instrument. The
drawer, the pin, the runway strips, the lead cover and the walk are not
touched.
