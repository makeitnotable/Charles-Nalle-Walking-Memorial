## 2 · The map page as shipped — the final version

On a phone whose browser bars are glass over the page (iOS 26 Safari; the
runways are zero wherever the large and small viewports are the same height,
so tablets and desktops are as v7 left them):

- **The document is taller than the screen by the two bars.** The map shell
  is `100svh + E + T`: T = the lesser of 110px and the bars' total height,
  E = the lesser of that total and 20svh (110 and 129 on Wil's phone). The
  canvas extends −T above and −E below the UI layer, whose own box is
  unchanged; the page opens scrolled to exactly T and returns there after
  load, orientation change, a bfcache return, or a scroll that settles (the
  `map.astro` landing script). So the live map lies under the glass of both
  bars, and the UI's controls never do. (Rounds 17–19; the geometry, the
  three traps and how to verify them without a phone are
  `docs/PLAYBOOK-MOBILE-CHROME.md`.)
- **Every open is the overview, with all five stops framed.** The camera fits
  the stops into a safe box measured in the canvas's coordinate space — T
  added, the hint chip and the card lane subtracted — and unprojects the
  centre through the same offset. Nothing writes `?stop=` any more; an
  inbound one is consumed and stripped; a bfcache return resets the walk.
  (Round 19.)
- **One lane for the walk door and the cards.** `--map-lane` (the safe-area
  inset + Wil's chosen −4px lift on phones; inset + 12px from 640px up)
  positions "Take the walk" and pads the card strip alike; the attribution
  row rides above it. While a stop is focused the page cannot scroll (wheel,
  touch, keys). Mapbox no longer re-fits on the bars' resize churn. (Rounds
  19–20.)
- **Safari's bars follow the section under them.** The edge sampler writes
  the section's colour to `<body>` on `/map` — the map's grey (#353535) at
  rest, the section brown once the brown section reaches the top edge — and
  Safari tints its bars from it. (Round 21; the body-colour rule is playbook
  §2.)
- **The 1858 lens is one see-through wash, bars included.** The lens shell
  covers the canvas box (−T to −E) with 70% black, keeps the plate's
  geometry with the runways as padding, declares its resulting colour over
  the map's ground (#101010) so `<body>` and the bars match, and leaves the
  Mapbox controls under the wash. (Rounds 27 and 29.)
