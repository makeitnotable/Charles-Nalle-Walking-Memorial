## 1 · The area behind the top bar follows the section

**His words.** "Set the area behind/around the browser's top UI (address bar /
toolbar / safe-area inset region) to a solid fill that matches the background
color of the section currently visible at the top of the viewport. The color
should update as the user scrolls between sections. Example: if the hero
section is currently visible, use the hero section's brown background color."
Three shots, all with the address bar minimized: the painting through the pill
over the hero; the heading ghosting through over the quote; the cream showing
through over the story with a dark band at the very top.

**Diagnosis.** Every chapter route handed Safari one static colour, the hero
brown `#100a06`, for the whole page (round 8 phase E, his instruction then).
Safari uses that colour only as a tint. Once the bar minimizes the layout
viewport starts below the pill (playbook §1: 645 at rest, 753 minimized, the
pair 199), and the region above it — status bar and pill — is glass over what
the document holds ABOVE the viewport's top edge: what has just scrolled past,
under a short gradient of `<body>`'s colour. That is all three shots at once.
The dark band over the cream is the brown gradient. Round 18's last attempt
(hypothesis 2: opaque strips at the edges, on screen, under `<main>`) did not
take — his shots are the result — so it is retired.

**What ships.**

- `<body>` follows the section at the top edge on the chapter routes — the
  round-21 rule from `/map`, gated on `data-top-bar="section"` as well as
  `data-root-fill="map"`. The resolver (`sectionGroundAt`) now counts `<main>`
  (the page ground since round 14) and `.ground-cream` blocks as grounds, and
  falls back to `<main>` before `<body>`: the story transcript and the history
  section paint their cream on a `<div>` inside the `<section>`, and the old
  walk fell past it to `<body>` — so over the cream the answer was the hero's
  brown (a latent bug in the Chrome-for-Android meta as well; fixed for both).
  The static `#100a06` rule stays as the at-rest and no-JS value; at scroll 0
  the sampler writes the same value, so nothing moves at rest.
- The **chapter cover**, `.edge-cover`: a 320px absolute box in the document
  (a child of `<body>`, in the scrolling layer, never fixed), moved on every
  scroll event so its BOTTOM edge rides on the viewport's top edge
  (`translateY = scrollY − 320`), coloured by the sampler with the same
  section colour. It covers only what has scrolled past — the reader sees it
  through the bar and nowhere else — which is the one region in-flow paint
  reaches and fixed paint does not (A2's clip; the map's runway). Its bottom
  16px are feathered by a mask: scrolling is asynchronous on iOS, so a fast
  upward flick can leave the cover a frame behind, and the feather turns a
  hard band into a faint haze under the pill. `display: none` unless
  `-webkit-touch-callout` is supported (the iOS gate the playbook records), so
  desktop, Android and the visual gate never see it and no listener is
  attached there. Z 800: over everything in `<main>`, under the walk rail, the
  mini-player and the menu. Device flags, inline and one page load:
  `?cover=off` (a before/after), `?feather=<px>` (0 = a hard edge).
- The chapter strips (round 18) are gone: the chapter routes no longer force
  any opaque extension at either edge. Nothing is painted behind the bottom
  toolbar — his answer to question 1.

**Residue, reported before shipping.** Safari has one tint for both bars, from
`<body>`. So when the bottom toolbar is expanded it carries the top section's
colour as a cast over whatever is under it — invisible while top and bottom
are in the same section, a faint mismatch across a section boundary. No
page-side lever separates the two bars; the page paints nothing there.

**What only the phone can show.** Whether Safari composites the cover through
the pill the way it composites the runway (the same in-flow mechanism, so it
should); how far a fast flick leaves the cover behind, and whether 16px of
feather hides it; how Safari's own tint animation (~300 ms) reads against the
cover's instant switch at a section boundary.

## 2 · The corner menu holds its place

**His words.** "The menu animates by sliding from the right to the left and
from up to down slightly when the user scrolls. (It's like its position is
getting pushed by the scroll bar.) On scroll up the menu should fade in; on
scroll down the menu should fade out. Do not use a slide-in/translate-from-
right animation for this menu."

**Diagnosis.** It was never animated. `--ui-inset` is one lane for every
corner and takes the deepest of the four safe areas; `env(safe-area-inset-
bottom)` is 0 with the bars expanded and 34px once they collapse (playbook
§1). So the top-right burger jumped from 20px to 34px on both axes — 14px down
and 14px left — on every scroll down and back on every scroll up, in the same
moment its retreat fade ran. The playbook listed this shift as open, by his
decision; this is the decision.

**What ships.** The top-right menu reads its own `--menu-inset`: the gutter,
the top inset and the right inset — its own two edges — and nothing at the
bottom edge can move it. The fade is unchanged (opacity plus the small settle
it already has; his answer to question 3). Every page that shows the menu
places it top-right (the map moved there in round 1), so this is the
site-wide behaviour he asked for. No other corner control changes: the
mini-player and the interlude credit still ride `--ui-inset` (RUN-STATE, open).

## 3 · The hero lockup sits on the gutter

**His words.** "On mobile (and tablet if the bottom toolbar is present), move
the hero section's text on all five chapter pages (excluding only part 2 of
chapter 2) down so that the vertical spacing between the bottom of the chapter
title and the top of the bottom toolbar feels optically the same as the
horizontal spacing between the left of the hero section text elements and the
left edge of the screen."

**Diagnosis.** The hero is one small viewport tall, so at rest its bottom edge
is the toolbar's top. The lockup's inset was `max(5svh, 32px)` — 32px on his
phone — plus the title line box's air under the baseline (Libre Caslon
Display at `--lh-display` 0.95: `(0.95 − 0.970 + 0.266) / 2` = 0.123em, 6px at
the phone size): the eye read ~38px against a 20px gutter.

**What ships.** On phones (below 768) the shell's inset is `var(--gutter)` and
the title's negative bottom margin takes the line-box slack out — ink to ink,
not box to box: on the left the eye measures the gutter plus the capital's
side bearing (~0.05em), so the margin removes 0.123 − 0.05 = 0.073em and the
last line's ink sits the same distance above the edge as the first letter's
ink sits from the left. On tablets (768–1023) the same, but only in a browser
with collapsing bars: `100lvh − 100svh` is the bars' height there and 0 on an
iPad and every desktop, and a trig cast turns that length into a 0/1 switch
(`clamp(0, tan(atan2(100lvh − 100svh, 1px)), 1)`), behind an `@supports` gate
so an older browser keeps the old inset rather than losing it. Desktop (1024
and up) and Commissioner's Part-2 hero (`#hero-2`) are untouched.
