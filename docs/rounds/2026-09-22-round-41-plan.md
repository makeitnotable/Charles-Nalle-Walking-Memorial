## 1 · What the screenshot says

Measured at 3x (1170px wide = 390 CSS px): the burger's right edge sits
20px from the screen's edge — its inset — and its top ~8px below the rail.
Three facts close the question of which of the two moved:

- the burger is a 72px box straight inside `.cnwm-menu`, no offset;
- its inset is `max(gutter, top inset, right inset)`, which cannot fall
  below the gutter (20px);
- its retreat transform is `translateY(12px)`, downward only.

So the menu had not moved. The rail had: ~12px below the viewport's top,
which is its `top: env(safe-area-inset-top)` (v13, for the home-screen
app). iOS 26 reports a top inset once its bars have moved in a scrolling
document; the still document never scrolls, so on the approved pages the
inset stays 0 and the menu sits one gutter below the rail. In the edge mode
the rail followed the inset and the menu did not — "the wrong place", and
the two drifting apart on every bar transition, "a flicker at the bottom
right underneath the progress bars".

The band "underneath the progress indicators": the trigger strip was 8px
and the rail covers only its first 3, so whatever treatment Safari gives
the fixed element it finds at the edge showed as a 5px band under the
stripe.

The size change is Safari's bar collapse itself, which is the toolbar
hiding; it stays in B.

## 2 · What ships, behind the flag (still)

- **The menu follows the rail.** In the edge mode only, the menu's top is
  the gutter below the same inset the rail rides —
  `calc(env(safe-area-inset-top) + max(gutter, right inset))`. At inset 0
  this is round 23's rule byte for byte; at any inset the menu is one gutter
  below the rail, the relation he approved, and the two move together
  instead of apart. The right inset is unchanged.
- **The strip is the rail's own 3px, behind it** (z 899 under 900): the
  probe still finds it at the viewport's top edge, and nothing of it shows
  below the stripe. `&band=<px>` still makes it a visible band.
- **Off the flag: nothing.** The default is the still document; both rules
  key on `data-scroll="edge"`.
