## Diagnosis (measured against the code at the base)

- **Items 2 and 3 are one defect.** Since v16 the shell carries a runway of
  `T = min(110px, 100lvh − 100svh)` above the UI layer and the page lands at
  scroll T. The runway is ordinary scrollable content, and three paths park the
  page at 0 instead: a flick back to the top; the island's own
  `scrollTo(0)` in `focusStop` and `bringShellIntoView` (pre-T code, never
  retargeted); a back/forward-cache restore. At 0 the whole composition sits T
  lower — pill down 110px, door under the toolbar, grey body fallback behind the
  address bar. That is screenshot 1 exactly.
- **Item 1's framing (screenshot 3 vs 6) is a coordinate-space defect in
  `overviewCamera()`.** `map.project` / `map.unproject` are container pixels,
  and since v16 the container's top edge is T above the UI layer, but the
  label safe box and the re-centring target were still written in window
  pixels. So the box sat T too high, every re-centring pass moved the group
  by T instead of converging, no zoom satisfied the loop at any pitch, and the
  phone branch fell through to the blind `OVERVIEW` constant — zoom 15.25 on
  the raw centroid, pins 2 and 5 off the top. Deterministic, not "sometimes".
  The fit also read `window.innerHeight`, which is 645 or 753 on his phone
  depending on the bars' state at load.
- **Item 1's persistence:** `?stop=` written on every focus and card settle
  (v7 V7-095, on purpose then) replays the arrival flight on reload and Back;
  a bfcache restore keeps the island alive as left, including the curtain's
  `leavingRef` latch, which silently disables the walk and the route draw.
- **Item 4:** with the shell in `svh` nothing resizes on a bar move, but
  `mapbox-gl` 3.27 listens to `window` resize itself and re-runs resize +
  render on every bar transition; the island rewrites all five pills 200 ms
  after each; and `--ui-inset` follows `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` (0 ↔ 34,
  measured in round 8), so every corner control moves 14px per transition.
  Only the first two are changed here — the inset is a site-wide design token.
