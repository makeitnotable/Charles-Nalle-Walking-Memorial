## 2 · What broke it

On 9/22 he asked for one more thing: "the bottom toolbar should automatically
hide when the user scrolls down and reappear when the user scrolls up. No
other changes should be made beyond this." Safari collapses its bars only
when the document scrolls, and the fill he had approved exists only while
the document is still — the trade pass 8 stated and he accepted on 9/19.
The session then rebuilt the chapter pages five times in one day, each push
live on `v2`, each on a hypothesis about Safari's bars that the phone did
not confirm:

| Pass | What went live | His read |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | The runway: the document scrolls a solid colour, the chapter rides in a fixed, clipped `<main>` moved by −scrollY; the mini player portaled to `<body>`; a `#reader` wrapper in the layout | The toolbar still not hiding (the gate had read iOS's pre-meta 980px width) |
| 14 | The gate reads the screen's shorter side | "This is closer" — the rail gone once scrolled, a brown fill under the toolbar over the cream |
| 15 | The rail through a `chrome` slot outside `<main>`; the runway a colour map of the chapter with a cap above the viewport | The toolbar still an opaque brown over cream |
| 16 | The page ground moved to `#reader`, `<main>` transparent | "Literally nothing has changed in the last two tries … start fresh" |
| 17 | A plain document plus one fixed, invisible, hit-testable 8px strip at the top edge (`.edge-trigger`), on the inference that Safari paints a bar opaque when its edge probe hits something inside a fixed box | Not read — this round instead |

The session's own record shows the compaction: the passes' verifications are
all Chromium probes with the gate stood in, and every "not verifiable here"
line names the one thing that mattered — the phone. Pass 17 in particular
turned the chapter pages back into a plain scrolling document with nothing
behind the top bar but a hypothesis: if the strip does not make Safari paint
the bar, the page is exactly the 9/18 state his three screenshots complain
about — the painting through the pill, the heading ghosting, the cream with
a dark band.
