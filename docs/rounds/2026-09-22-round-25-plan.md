## 2 · What ships

**The cover.** An opaque page-ground box inside the lead box, over the
painting (`.museum-lead-cover`, paintings.astro). Three rules, global.css:

- It leaves on its own 2.4s after the page's styles apply — a 300ms fade at
  the house curve (`--dur-fast`, `--ease`), `animation-fill-mode: both` so
  it is opaque through the wait. A capable phone goes brown → hall and never
  sees the painting; a reader whose island never runs sees the page ground
  for 2.4s and then the painting. 2.4s is a slow phone's mount with room to
  spare; a mount later than that shows the painting for the remainder —
  today's behaviour, shorter.
- It leaves at once when the island runs and finds the hall cannot
  (Museum.tsx's capability gate: no WebGL, reduced motion, a thin
  connection, a lost context): the island marks the slot `data-hall="off"`
  and the cover is `display: none`. The painting is the page for that
  reader and appears as soon as the page knows — measured 157ms here.
- It never exists without JS (no `.js` on `<html>`) or under reduced motion
  (`prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` gate): the painting IS the page
  there, and the fade is motion.

**Why the painting is not faded itself.** Largest Contentful Paint ignores
an element at opacity 0 and counts it only once it is visible; hiding the
painting would move the page's LCP candidate 2.4s later and put the M1
budget (LCP < 2.5s throttled) at risk. Covered, it still paints beneath the
cover at the same moment as before, and LCP does not see occlusion. So the
preloads, the `fetchpriority`, the image itself are untouched.

Files: `src/pages/paintings.astro` (the cover div, a `museum-lead` class on
the lead box), `src/styles/global.css` (the round-25 block),
`src/components/Museum.tsx` (one line: `data-hall="off"` when the gate says
no), `scripts/museum-lead.mjs` + `package.json` (`qa:lead`), and the docs
in the manifest. Round 24's record takes his approval (its plan, playbook
§11, RUN-STATE).
