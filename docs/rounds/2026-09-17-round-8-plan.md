## 3. How Safari 26 behaves: known vs unknown

### Known (multiple sources agree)

- **`theme-color` is ignored.** The bars are tinted from the `<body>` background (then `<html>`), and from `position: fixed/sticky` elements near the edges. The WebKit observer updates the tint live as colours change.
- **Qualifying elements.** An element counts when it is:
  - within about 4px of the top, or about 3px of the bottom on iOS (partially off-screen still counts: `bottom: -8px` with `min-height: 12px` was sampled);
  - at least 80% wide on iOS, and at least 90% wide on macOS;
  - at least 3px tall (6px or more on the Mac, per bleedblend).
- **Still read:** `opacity: 0`, `visibility: hidden` and `pointer-events: none` elements.
- **Not read:** `display: none`.
- **Also not read (reported):** `::before`/`::after` on fixed elements, `position: absolute` children of fixed elements, and elements with `backdrop-filter`.
- **Ordinary sections at the edge are sampled too**, not just fixed ones (bleedblend).
- **A 12px strip** at the edge with the wanted colour is a working way to force a tint (bleedblend).
- **iOS Chrome-style `transparent` quirk.** WebKit treats the `transparent` keyword as black at alpha 0, which can band during transitions. Write `rgba(r,g,b,0)` instead.
- **Fixed full-viewport images stop at the bars.** On iOS 26 they "do not fill the screen": two background-coloured spaces remain top and bottom, while content that scrolls is visible behind the bars (Apple developer forum 809407). This matches the corridor report exactly.

### Unknown, and contradicted between sources (Phase A decides)

- **U1.** In Wil's **Top** layout, can the **expanded** bars (at rest) ever show page content? Or does the expanded state always draw a tint band, with content showing only when the bars minimize while scrolling?
- **U2.** What does a **semi-transparent** or **fully clear** edge element do? Sources disagree:
  - Jahir says a semi-transparent fixed overlay made the toolbar transparent;
  - bleedblend says natural translucency needs *no* edge element (`display: none`).

  Wil's reload result suggests round 7's near-clear strips produce a flat page-brown band.
- **U3.** Is the curtain's `::before` colour picked up during a transition, and why does the bottom tint then stay stale on the next page?
- **U4.** Does content inside a **sticky** element that overflows its box (the corridor bleed) render under the bars?
- **U5.** How tall are the covered regions (top and bottom), at rest and while scrolled, in Wil's Top layout and in Compact? CSS cannot report them: `env(safe-area-inset-top)` reads 0, and `100lvh − 100svh` is only the sum of both bars.
- **U6.** Does an opaque, `opacity: 0` top strip win over the chapter pages' 3px transparent `.walk-rail`, both at rest and with the bar minimized?

## 4. Diagnosis

- **`/map`.** Nothing but brown sits behind the bars:
  - the map ends where the toolbar starts, and the index below is brown;
  - above the page top there is nothing.

  Even perfect glass would show brown. The map must physically extend under the bottom toolbar (Wil's decision), and whether that is *visible* at rest depends on U1 and U2.
- **`/paintings` bottom at rest after a menu transition.** This is a stale `#100a06` tint from the curtain (U3). After a reload, the bottom band is page brown even though the corridor canvas is physically under the toolbar at rest (the stage runs from about 423pt to about 1068pt on an 844pt screen). That points at U1 or U2: the edge candidates (the round-7 strips at `--edge-glass`, and the sticky stage's own `--edge-glass` background) produce a flat band instead of glass.
- **`/paintings` while walking.** The stage is pinned inside the layout viewport, so nothing is under either bar except the empty museum slot (brown). The canvas must extend beyond the stage box while stuck (U4, U5).
- **Chapter pages.** Top glass is visible in the minimized state today. Wil wants a solid, section-coloured fill instead. An invisible opaque top strip is the documented mechanism; U6 is the risk.

### Phase B — the fixes (decided by the lab)

#### 5.1 Chapter pages: solid, section-coloured address bar

- **Scope:** the five chapter routes only. Add a `Base.astro` prop (e.g. `topBar="section"`) set by `[chapter].astro`; it sets `data-top-bar="section"` on `<html>`.
- **Strips (site-wide):** 12px tall, **no longer gated** behind `@supports (-webkit-touch-callout: none)`, so Mac Safari gets them too. They remain `opacity: 0`, `pointer-events: none` and never hit-tested.
- **The top colour:** extend the existing sampler in `Base.astro` (same schedule, same 100ms throttle, so no new per-frame cost). When `data-top-bar="section"` is set, it computes `sectionGroundAt(4)`:
  1. From the top of the `elementsFromPoint(w/2, 4)` stack, find the nearest ancestor-or-self that is a `<header>`, `<section>`, `<footer>` or a direct child of `<main>` **and** has an opaque `background-color`.
  2. Otherwise, use `<body>`'s.
  3. `data-edge-*` declarations are **ignored** here (they are artwork colours).
  4. Over the hero, this must resolve to **`#100a06`** (`header … bg-neutral-2`). Verify it; if it does not, special-case the hero.
- **Writing the colour:**
  - It goes to `.edge-glass-top` as an inline `background-color` (opaque).
  - The bottom strip stays as it is.
  - **Chrome for Android:** on these pages only, the `theme-color` meta takes the same top colour (Wil asked for this on every device with a bar). The two-edge "best" logic is unchanged on every other page.
- **If T4 shows the rail blocks the strip:** do not alter the rail's appearance. Report to Wil with options.
- **If T4 fails outright:** stop and report.

#### 5.2 `/paintings`: bottom glass, and the corridor under both bars while walking

1. **Stale curtain tint.** Adopt whichever T7 variant leaves no stale tint. It must stay pixel-identical during and after transitions: the same cover, label and timing.
2. **Edge candidates on `/paintings`.** Set the strips and the stage's own background to the combination T1–T3 and T5 showed as see-through. Keep the stage's visible ground on `::before` exactly as round 7 did.
3. **Corridor bleed while stuck** (iOS/iPadOS only, feature-detected; X = Y = 0 everywhere else, so desktop and Android render exactly as today):
   - **Extension.** The rendered corridor extends **X above and Y below the stage box**, where X and Y are CSS custom properties set from T6's measurements plus about 20% margin. Use the larger of the Top and Compact values, because the layout cannot be detected.
   - **When it shows.** The bleed shows **only while the stage is stuck.** At rest and after release, the page must be pixel-identical, so the upward bleed must never paint over the header or the `mt-16` gap, and the downward bleed must never paint over the stills section. Drive a class from the Museum's existing scroll state; add no new scroll listeners.
   - **Framing.** Keep the stage-box band pixel-identical using `camera.setViewOffset(W, H, 0, -X, W, H + X + Y)` (or equivalent). The projection of the visible band must equal today's.
   - **Unchanged:**
     - every overlay (SKIP, SCROLL TO WALK, dots, counter, approach sheet);
     - **raycasting and pointer maths**, which must map from the stage box, not the enlarged canvas box;
     - resize and orientation behaviour.
   - **Performance.** The canvas grows by X + Y pixels of height. Measure median and p95 frame time before and after (Chromium, bleed forced on, 4× CPU throttle, the same scripted walk). Report the numbers. If the median rises more than 15%, stop and ask Wil. The alternative — a lower pixel ratio for the same pixel count — is a visible sharpness change and needs his sign-off.

#### 5.3 `/map`: the map under the bottom toolbar

**Gate.** Do this only if T1–T3 show the **expanded** bottom bar can reveal content in **Top or Compact**.
- If only Compact can, say so to Wil: on his phone the list would move with no visible gain.
- Proceed only on his yes.

**Requirements:**
- **Where it applies.** Only where a bottom toolbar exists: iPhone portrait (feature-detect iOS and use a portrait / narrow media query). E = 0 everywhere else. E is **static**, from T6's at-rest bottom reading plus about 20% margin; never `dvh` (the v14.3 jitter note).
- **Shell height:** `.map-shell` becomes `calc(100svh + E)`. **Accepted consequence:** the index below starts E lower.
- **UI position.** Every element anchored to the shell's bottom moves up by E so its screen position is unchanged:
  - the hint (`TroyMap.tsx` about line 1800);
  - `.map-scroll-handle` (about 1873);
  - the *Take the walk* wrapper (about 1885);
  - the Mapbox bottom controls (`.mapboxgl-ctrl-bottom-left/right`, `global.css` about 1253), where the logo and attribution **must stay visible** (Mapbox terms).
- **Unchanged:** the fixed card strip (layout-viewport anchored) and top-anchored UI.
- **Camera framing.** Keep it identical by adding E to Mapbox's bottom padding: `map.setPadding` or the padding passed to `cameraForBounds` / `fitBounds` / `easeTo`. Check how per-call padding combines with map padding in this Mapbox version.
  - Audit every `window.innerHeight` and `box.clientHeight` use (about lines 148, 266, 297, 348, 629, 839, 981, 1044, 1064) so none changes because of E.
  - Walk mode, focus mode and the lens must behave as today.
