## 1 · The exception, and the detail, as told

- **The top bar will shrink and grow.** The toolbar hides only when the
  document scrolls, and Safari moves the top bar in the same animation.
  Its fill can stay exactly as approved — solid, the section's colour,
  switching at each boundary — but "zero changes to the top bar" is not
  available to a page. Stated; accepted ("Fine!").
- **The toolbar's fill.** In the runway as it ran on his phone (passes
  15–16), the fill behind the returning toolbar was the page's brown, not
  the section's colour — Safari paints a bar opaque over a fixed element at
  its edge (round 24's rule) and did so whatever the runway held. Whether
  that fill follows `<body>`'s colour (the section at the top edge, which
  the sampler writes) is what his phone will show; brown is the certain
  case.
- **One frame.** The chapter is placed by the main thread one frame behind
  the finger; a stalled main thread stalls the page with it. He saw this
  in passes 14–16 and did not remark on it.

## 2 · What ships, behind the flag

Round 23's pass-13 runway, re-applied from its own diff and adapted:

- **The flag.** `?scroll=sync` on a chapter route; the head script's default
  branch still assigns `"inner"`, so every iOS chapter is the still document
  he approved until his read says otherwise.
- **The runway.** `<main>` is `position: fixed`, `100lvh` tall and
  `overflow: clip` (a clip, not a scroller, so touches fall through to the
  document); `#reader` — a wrapper around `<main>`'s content, an unstyled
  block off the flag — is translated by −scrollY on every scroll event and
  re-read for 600 ms after the last (the last scroll event is not where iOS
  settles); `<body>` is sized to the chapter by script and carries the
  section's colour from the sampler, which is what shows behind the top
  bar (solid, static, switching at each section) and what Safari's opaque
  fill takes for the returning toolbar; a `#hash` is landed by scrolling
  the document to the target's natural offset.
- **Everything pinned leaves the moving box.** The walk rail is moved out
  of `#reader` at runtime by the sync script, after `<main>` — pass 15
  found it riding away with the page; done at runtime rather than through
  a slot so the default's DOM is untouched. The narration's mini player is
  rendered into `<body>` through a portal in this mode only (pass 13 did it
  in every mode). The corner menu and the cover already live outside
  `<main>`.
- **The menu on the rail's inset** (round 41's rule, this mode only): in a
  scrolling document iOS 26 reports a top inset once its bars have moved,
  the rail rides it, and the menu's `max()` did not — 12px apart on his
  phone. Here the menu's top is the gutter below the same inset.
- **Not brought back.** Pass 15's colour map and cap (the toolbar was
  opaque anyway) and pass 16's transparent `<main>`; the cover and the
  visor stay `doc`-only as pass 13 left them.
- **Off the flag:** one unstyled wrapper div in the DOM, and nothing else.
