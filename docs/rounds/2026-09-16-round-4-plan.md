## Item-by-item decisions

### 1 · Museum drawer (tablet + phone) — WP-B
`.museum-sheet` radius 24px → **32px** (the drawer alone; `--r-lg` is shared by
the hero/home frames and does not move), the head focus ring follows. Stroke:
`1px solid var(--color-primary-6)` (#69311d, the palette's designated hairline,
one step quieter than the primary-7 frame E12 removed) on top, left and right;
bottom open (off-screen; a line there reads as a seam). The body's max-height
regains the `- 1px` the border costs. The desktop/landscape `.museum-card`
stays borderless (not in the ask). Peek-title ink offsets re-measured to round
3's ±1px rule.

### 2 · Dot rail air — WP-B
Both gaps in the bottom-centre column — Face Forward ↔ dots and dots ↔ counter
— 12px → **16px** (`--sp-2`, the next spacing step) at every breakpoint.
Bottom offset unchanged.

### 3 · Scroll-reversal jitter on phones — WP-E
Reversal on iOS is when Safari's bars move; every in-flow stage sized in `dvh`
re-lays out by the bar delta above the reader (the shake), and full-screen
media resizes with it (the freeze). The fix is the smallest design-preserving
one the measurements indict (see the agent's table in the round report);
sticky/fixed stages (map shell, museum stage) keep `dvh`. Never `lvh`, never a
bar token. **Device-first: unverified on iOS here; goes to Wil's phone on the
review branch before `v2`.**

### 4 · Card strip edge opacity (tablet + desktop) — WP-C
The ≥1024 120px eased mask and the anti-sliver fade (<25% visible → 0) are
what ghost an outermost card. New profile: hold ≥ ~0.85 until the last ~40–48px
before the frame edge, then an eased ramp to 0; the anti-sliver rule starts
later (≈12%) and stays continuous. Phones (<640) keep no mask.

### 5 · Map scroll-handle arrow — WP-C
The `<svg>` inside `.map-scroll-handle` (phone-only band under "Take the
Walk") is removed; the invisible band stays as the touch scroll affordance.
No other instance of the glyph exists.

### 6 · Browser chrome — WP-A
Remove `.chrome-tint-top/-bottom` (markup, CSS, the two sampler outputs).
Keep `theme-color` + sampler (fallback 3 for Chrome for Android), keep
`viewport-fit=cover`, `color-scheme: dark`, the safe-area insets. No web API
forces Safari's floating (compact) bar — it is a user setting; the page can
only stop painting bands under it. `chrome-test.astro` loses its strip
toggles; `bleed-check.mjs`'s TINT rule follows. **Device-first: Safari's bar
colour after the removal is unverifiable here.**

### 7 · Splash bottom inset — WP-D
`index.astro`: phones 56 → **28px**, 768–1023 64 → **48px** (the
`max(…, env(safe-area-inset-bottom))` form stays). Landscape tier and desktop
untouched.

### 8 · Button text — WP-D
`.btn, .btn-sm`: `font-weight` 700 → **400**; `text-transform: uppercase`
removed. Every `.btn`/`.btn-sm` label authored in Title Case (first/last word
and every word except articles, coordinating conjunctions and short
prepositions). Tracking (0.08em, tuned for caps) is left as Wil did not ask;
flagged in the report. Not buttons, not touched: `.t-meta` pills, map pins,
chips, menu labels, aria-labels, curtain labels.
