## The caption/label conflict on the Martin drawing — resolved by picture

The 1920² pen whose burned-in label reads "Rushing the Room, Pen on paper,
8½" × 7⅛", 2008" is captioned "Martin Struck by Deputy Sheriff Morrison"
on the series page. `docs/v11/PAINTING-NAMES.md` §2 took the burned-in label
as truth. Re-examined 8/25 against both acrylics at full resolution, the
drawing matches the MARTIN painting element for element — the central figure
pinned at the door from both sides, the raised weapon, the torn WANTED sheet
top-right, and above all the two giant foreshortened fallen heads filling
the bottom edge, which the Rushing-the-Room acrylic does not have. The
burned-in label is the artist's own mislabel; the caption is correct.
Consequence: **Rushing the Room (`barbershop/narrative1`) has NO study
anywhere on the series page** — one is needed from Mark Priest, or that
work hangs without one.
