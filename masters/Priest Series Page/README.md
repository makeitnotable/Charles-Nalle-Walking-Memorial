## The identification the series page gets wrong

The 1920² plate whose burned-in label reads **"Rushing the Room, Pen on
paper, 8½" × 7⅛", 2008"** is captioned *"Martin Struck by Deputy Sheriff
Morrison"* on the series page. `docs/v11/PAINTING-NAMES.md` §2 followed the
caption. An 8/25 reading of the plates alone reached the same conclusion, and
**both were wrong**: Wil's folder supplies TWO drawings here — `9. Sketch of
Rushing the Room` (this plate, cropped) and `10. Sketch of Martin Felled by
Axe` (a different drawing the series page never showed). With both in hand the
simple reading holds: **the artist's burned-in label is right and the series
page's caption is the error.** Wil's map is authoritative and consistent with
the labels; nothing here contradicts it.
